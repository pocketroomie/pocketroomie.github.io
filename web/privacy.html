Privacy Policy

Roomie, for iPhone · Last updated November 23, 2026

Roomie collects no data. Everything it uses stays on your iPhone. Roomie has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it doesn't send anything to us or to anyone else.

What Roomie uses, and why

  • Your Home (HomeKit): to show and control the accessories you set up in Apple's Home app, room by room. Roomie reads and controls them directly on your iPhone through HomeKit. HomeKit data is never used for advertising or shared with anyone.
  • Bluetooth: only in Auto mode. Roomie measures the signal of nearby devices to tell which room you're in, and sets up Roomie beacons. The measurements stay on your iPhone.
  • Location (While Using): only if you turn on room beacons in Auto mode, because iOS detects beacons through Location Services. Roomie doesn't use your location or GPS, and it doesn't run in the background.

What Roomie stores

Your settings (detection mode, favorite rooms, hidden accessories), your rooms' Bluetooth fingerprints (what Roomie learned about each room) and beacon assignments are saved on your iPhone only. Deleting Roomie, or using Settings → About → Reset All Roomie Data, removes them.

Roomie beacons

The optional Roomie beacon firmware for ESP32 boards uses Bluetooth only. It has no Wi-Fi or internet connection and stores nothing but its own beacon settings. The firmware and the installer page don't collect any data.

Children

Roomie collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
