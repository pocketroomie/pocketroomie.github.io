Pocket Roomie

Privacy Policy

Pocket Roomie, the room beacon firmware and this website · Last updated November 25, 2026

Pocket Roomie doesn't collect your data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no network connection of its own. What it learns about your home stays on your iPhone, and nothing is sent to the developers or to anyone else.

Who this covers

This policy covers (1) the Pocket Roomie app, (2) the optional Pocket Roomie beacon firmware for ESP32 boards, and (3) this website. "We" means the developers of Pocket Roomie (the Pocket Roomie project). You can reach us through the link under "Contact" below.

The Pocket Roomie app

What it uses, and why

  • Your Home (HomeKit): to show and control the accessories you set up in Apple's Home app, room by room. Pocket Roomie talks to HomeKit directly on your iPhone. HomeKit information is never used for advertising, and never leaves your iPhone through Pocket Roomie.
  • Bluetooth: only in Auto mode, and when you set up room beacons. Pocket Roomie measures the signal strength and advertising patterns of Bluetooth devices around you to recognize which room you are in. This is processed on your iPhone only. Pocket Roomie does not try to identify people or what the devices are.
  • Location (While Using): only if you turn on room beacons in Auto mode, because iOS detects beacons through Location Services. Pocket Roomie does not read or store your coordinates, and it does not run in the background.

What it saves on your iPhone

  • Your settings: detection mode, favorite rooms, hidden accessories, icon choices, switching speed.
  • A copy of the names, rooms and last known states of your accessories, so Pocket Roomie opens quickly.
  • What Pocket Roomie learned about each room: Bluetooth "fingerprints" (signal patterns, not names) and which beacon belongs to which room.
  • Optional Scan Lab recordings, if you make any: nearby Bluetooth advertising data, saved as files on your iPhone.

All of this is stored only on your iPhone. It may be included in your device backups (for example iCloud Backup or a computer backup), under your own Apple account's settings and Apple's terms. Deleting Pocket Roomie, or using Settings → About → Reset All Pocket Roomie Data, removes it from the iPhone.

Sharing is always your choice

Pocket Roomie never uploads anything. If you export or share a Scan Lab file, it goes only where you send it. Such a file can contain identifiers of Bluetooth devices around you, some of which belong to other people, so share it carefully.

Apple's services

HomeKit, iCloud, backups, Siri, Shortcuts and the App Store are provided by Apple under Apple's own terms and privacy policy. We don't receive information from them about you.

The room beacon firmware

The optional firmware for ESP32 boards uses Bluetooth only. It has no Wi-Fi or internet connection, and stores nothing but its own beacon settings (its identifiers, transmit power and name). It collects no data.

This website

  • No cookies, no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, and no fonts or scripts loaded from other sites. Everything the page uses, including the installer, is served from this site.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives technical information when you visit (such as your IP address, browser and the pages requested) and may keep server logs. We don't receive or use that information. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.
  • The installer talks to your board over USB, from your browser, using your browser's Web Serial feature. Your browser asks your permission first. Nothing about your board is sent to us.
  • Links to other sites (GitHub and others) are governed by those sites' own policies.

What we can't see, so can't share

Because Pocket Roomie doesn't send data to us, we hold no personal data about you. That means there is nothing for us to sell, share, disclose to authorities or lose in a data breach, and nothing for us to access, correct, delete or export on request. The data on your iPhone is yours, and you control it with iOS and Pocket Roomie's reset option. Whatever rights you have under your local law (for example the GDPR or CCPA) apply to the parties that actually hold data, such as Apple and GitHub.

Children

Pocket Roomie doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children. Pocket Roomie controls home accessories, so it is meant for use by adults, or by children under adult supervision.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. If a future version of Pocket Roomie ever collected data, this policy would say so before that version was released.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/pocketroomie/pocketroomie.github.io/issues. Please don't post private information in a public issue.
