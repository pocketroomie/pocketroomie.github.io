Roomie
Light · Simple · Quick

Your Apple Home,
one room at a time.

Roomie is a companion to Apple's Home app. It doesn't change anything: it uses the lights and devices you've already set up, and simply shows you the room you're in, so a lamp is always one tap away.

It knows the room you're in

Open Roomie and it's already on the right room: just its lamps, blinds and fans, big and easy to tap. Walk through your home once so it learns your rooms, and it follows you from there.

Another room in a swipe

Swipe in from the edge of the screen and your favorite rooms appear right under your thumb: slide onto one and you're there, with one hand, from either side. Or press the iPhone's Action button to open Roomie where you need it.

See what's still on

Going to bed or leaving the house? What's On lists every light and device left on, anywhere at home, and turns them all off with one tap.

Changes nothingYour rooms, accessories and automations stay exactly as they are in the Home app. Roomie just gives them a lighter, quicker face.
Nothing to set upNo accounts, no new hubs, nothing to install around the house. If it's in the Home app, it's in Roomie.
PrivateNo ads, no tracking, no data collected. Everything stays on your iPhone.

Even more precise, if you like

Roomie learns your rooms from the devices you already have. But rooms next to each other, or open to each other like a kitchen and a living room, can look alike. A small beacon in one of them tells Roomie exactly which side of the doorway you're on, and keeps teaching it your rooms as your home changes. Completely optional.

Add beacons

Any Bluetooth beacon you can buy works, from any brand. Put it in the room and pick its room in Roomie. That's it.

Or build your own

Got an ESP32 board, or happy to buy an inexpensive one? Install Roomie's free beacon software from your computer in a couple of minutes, with no settings to type.
