How to use Pocket Roomie

That's all there is to it.

Pocket Roomie is made to need no manual. Here is everything, once, in order.

Connect your Home

Open Pocket Roomie and tap Connect. iOS asks once for permission to your Home, so Pocket Roomie can show the lights and devices you already set up in the Home app.

Nothing is copied or sent anywhere. If you tap Not Now, you can allow it later in the iPhone's Settings.

Choose how it finds your room

Manual needs nothing: you pick a room and it stays until you change it. Auto follows you from room to room, using the Bluetooth signals already around your home.

To set up Auto, open Settings → Auto Setup and scan each room 3 times, 15 seconds each, standing in a different spot every time. Switching speed lets you choose between quick (Fast) and steady (Careful). Change your mind any time.

Use your room

Tap a tile to switch it. − and + dim a light, or move blinds. Pocket Roomie asks the Home app, so what you see is what your home is doing.

Press and hold a tile for everything about that accessory: brightness, colour, white tone, and more for fans, blinds or thermostats. Scroll down to choose its icon from a big set, or to hide it. Pocket Roomie keeps those choices on your iPhone; your Home app stays exactly as it is.

Change room in a swipe

Swipe in from the edge of the screen and your favorite rooms appear under your thumb. Slide onto one and let go. You can also tap a room's name at the top.

Choose which rooms appear in Settings → Quick Menu.

See what's still on

What's On lists every light and device left on, in every room, with the room you're in first. Tap one to turn it off, or tap Turn All Off.

Open it from the first tab of the room names, or from the Action button, Control Center or the Lock Screen.

Room beacons

For even more accuracy.

Optional, and the best way to make Auto sharper. A small beacon in a room tells Pocket Roomie exactly which room you're in. Buy ready-made ones, or build your own further down.

Buy ready-made beacons Recommended

What to look for

  • A standard Bluetooth beacon, with a UUID, Major and Minor. Most small beacons are.
  • A maker's app that lets you change the Major and Minor, so each beacon can be different.
  • Adjustable transmit power is a big help: low power keeps a beacon in its own room.
  • Any brand works. Battery or USB powered is up to you; USB never needs a new battery.
  • One beacon per room is plenty. Start with the rooms Pocket Roomie mixes up.

Set them up

Give each one its own Major and Minor. Many beacons are sold with the same Major and Minor, so Pocket Roomie can't tell them apart. Set them with the maker's own app.

Add each UUID in Pocket Roomie under Settings → Auto Setup → Room Beacons → Add Beacon ID. Beacons can share one UUID or use different ones; Pocket Roomie takes as many as you add.

Lower the power as far as still reaches the far corner of the room. A strong beacon leaks into the next room and blurs the difference. After changing power or moving a beacon, scan the rooms again.

Build your own

Pocket Roomie supplies free firmware that turns an ESP32 board into a room beacon, in one click from your computer. ESP32 boards differ (antenna, power supply), so results can be mixed; ready-made beacons are the safer choice.

This browser can't talk to USB devices. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer. This page needs to be opened over HTTPS.

Works with ESP32, ESP32-C3, ESP32-S3, ESP32-C6 and ESP32-H2. The chip is detected automatically.

The firmware is open source (MIT). See the code.

How it works

  1. Plug the ESP32 into this computer with a USB cable that carries data (some only charge).
  2. Click Install Pocket Roomie Beacon, pick the board and confirm. Erase wipes any earlier setup.
  3. Plug it in where it will live, roughly in the middle of the room.
  4. On your iPhone, open Pocket Roomie → Settings → Auto Setup → Room Beacons and tap the new beacon (it appears as Roomie-XXXX) to pick its room.

Good to know

Board not found? Hold the BOOT button while plugging it in, then click Install again.

Changing a beacon later: unplug it and plug it back in. For 3 minutes it accepts changes from Pocket Roomie.

Private: Bluetooth only, no Wi-Fi. It connects to nothing and stores only its own settings.

Help

If something isn't right.

Pocket Roomie shows no rooms, or says it can't reach my Home

Open the iPhone's Settings → Pocket Roomie and make sure Home is on. Pocket Roomie also needs at least one room with an accessory in the Home app.

Auto shows the wrong room, or keeps switching

Scan the room again in 3 different spots: Settings → Auto Setup → the room → Record Scan. Rooms next to each other need scans from near their shared door.

Try Switching speed → Careful. If an old scan is the problem, use Forget Everything Learned in Auto Setup, then scan again.

Rooms open to each other, like a kitchen and a living room, look alike to Bluetooth. A room beacon in one of them settles it.

Auto doesn't change rooms at all

Auto needs Bluetooth on, and Location set to While Using if you use room beacons. It looks for your room while Pocket Roomie is open on screen.

A tile says "No response"

Pocket Roomie shows what your Home app knows. Open the Home app: if the accessory doesn't respond there either, check its power, its Wi-Fi and your home hub. Pull down in the room to refresh.

Controls don't work when I'm away from home

Controlling your home from outside needs a home hub, such as a HomePod or an Apple TV. It works the same as in the Home app.

Pocket Roomie can't find my room beacons, or mixes them up

Each beacon needs its own Major and Minor. Many are sold with the same ones, which makes them look like a single beacon. Change them with the maker's own app.

Every UUID your beacons use must be added in Settings → Auto Setup → Room Beacons → Add Beacon ID. You can add as many as you need.

Lower the transmit power so a beacon is strong only in its own room, then forget what Pocket Roomie learned and scan the rooms again.

I want to start again

Settings → Reset All Pocket Roomie Data clears everything Pocket Roomie keeps on your iPhone. Your Home app isn't touched.
