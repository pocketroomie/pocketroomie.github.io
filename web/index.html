Roomie
Light · Simple · Quick

Your Apple Home,
one room at a time.

Roomie is a companion to Apple's Home app. It doesn't change anything: it uses the lights and devices you've already set up, and simply shows you the room you're in, so a lamp is always one tap away.

It knows the room you're in

Open Roomie and it's already on the right room: just its lamps, blinds and fans, big and easy to tap. Walk through your home once so it learns your rooms, and it follows you from there.

2

Another room in a swipe

Swipe sideways for the next room, or in from the edge for your favorites. Or press the iPhone's Action button and Roomie opens right where you need it.

3

See what's still on

Going to bed or leaving the house? What's On lists every light and device left on, anywhere at home, and turns them all off with one tap.
