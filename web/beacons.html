Roomie

Build your own room beacon

Turn an inexpensive ESP32 board into a room beacon for Roomie. One click from your computer, no settings to type.

This browser can't talk to USB devices. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer. This page needs to be opened over HTTPS.

Works with ESP32, ESP32-C3, ESP32-S3, ESP32-C6 and ESP32-H2. The chip is detected automatically.

How it works

  1. Plug the ESP32 into this computer with a USB cable that carries data (some cables only charge).
  2. Click Install Roomie Beacon, pick the board in the list and confirm. Choosing erase wipes any previous setup.
  3. Plug the beacon in where it will live, roughly in the middle of the room.
  4. On your iPhone, open Roomie → Settings → Auto Setup, turn on Use Room Beacons, open Room Beacons and tap the new beacon (it appears as Roomie-XXXX) to pick its room.

Good to know

Board not found? Hold the BOOT button while plugging it in, then click Install again.

Changing a beacon later: unplug it and plug it back in. For 3 minutes it accepts changes from Roomie, then it's a plain beacon again.

Private: the beacon uses Bluetooth only (no Wi-Fi), connects to nothing and stores nothing but its own beacon settings.
